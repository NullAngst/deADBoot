# deADBoot do-not-touch list.
# Packages here cannot be selected in the Debloat tab and are refused by debloat recipe steps.
# Removing them bootloops, soft-bricks or locks you out of the phone.
# One package per line. Add your own in ~/.local/share/deadboot/debloat-protected.txt
# (Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\deADBoot\debloat-protected.txt). The default launcher and the
# keyboard in use are also protected at runtime, whatever their package name.
